The atelier

Slow flowers, made with intention

Noir & Peony began as a one-table studio with a love for dramatic, romantic floral design. We buy from small growers, work to order and never overstock, so every stem is fresh and every bouquet is personal.

Our florists listen first, then design. That is why our bouquets look the way they do, and why they feel like you.
